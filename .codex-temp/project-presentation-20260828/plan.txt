Communication job: By the end, computer science students should understand how this project connects Go rules, Monte Carlo tree search, a PyTorch policy/value network, and self-play training data into one AlphaZero-style prototype.

Narrative arc:
1. Establish the project goal and actual runtime configuration.
2. Follow one move through the system architecture.
3. Explain the board and rule primitives that make search possible.
4. Walk through selection, expansion, and backpropagation.
5. Show the Java/Python boundary and the state/pi/z training record.
6. Close with what is implemented, what is not yet trained, and the next engineering steps.

Visual direction: Codex Grid-inspired white canvas, black typography, pale gray structure, blue accent, flat composition, and varied technical layouts.
